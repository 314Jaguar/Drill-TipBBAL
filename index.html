Dribbling

Improve your dribbling skills with these tips and drills:

  • Dribble with both hands
  • Practice dribbling with your head up
  • Use cones to practice dribbling around obstacles
  • Work on your crossover, behind-the-back, and spin moves
  • Dribble at different speeds and heights
  • Practice dribbling with resistance bands
  • Use a tennis ball to improve hand-eye coordination
  • Perform stationary dribbling drills
  • Dribble two balls simultaneously to improve coordination

Fun Activity: Dribbling Relay Race

Set up a relay race with friends or teammates. Use cones to create a course and see who can dribble through it the fastest without losing control of the ball. This will improve your dribbling skills and add a competitive element to your practice.

Shooting

Enhance your shooting accuracy with these tips and drills:

  • Focus on your shooting form and follow-through
  • Practice shooting from different spots on the court
  • Work on your free throws and mid-range shots
  • Practice shooting off the dribble and off the catch
  • Use a shooting machine or partner for rebounding
  • Practice shooting with a defender in your face
  • Work on your three-point shots
  • Practice shooting under fatigue
  • Incorporate shooting drills into your daily routine

Fun Activity: Horse

Play a game of Horse with friends. Take turns trying to make challenging shots, and if one player makes a shot, the next player must replicate it. This will help you practice shooting from various spots and under different conditions.

Defense

Boost your defensive skills with these tips and drills:

  • Stay low and maintain a good defensive stance
  • Work on your lateral quickness and footwork
  • Practice closeouts and contesting shots
  • Improve your ability to read the offense
  • Communicate with your teammates on defense
  • Practice defensive slides
  • Work on your shot-blocking timing
  • Improve your rebounding techniques
  • Watch film to understand defensive strategies

Fun Activity: Defensive Slide Tag

Play a game of tag where players can only move using defensive slides. This will improve your lateral quickness, footwork, and ability to maintain a low defensive stance while having fun.

Plays

Learn and practice popular basketball plays:

Screens

Setting effective screens and using them to create scoring opportunities.

Fun Activity: Screen and Roll Challenge

Practice setting screens and executing the pick-and-roll play with a partner. Create a scoring competition to see who can score the most points using this play in a set time.

Fast Breaks

Executing fast breaks for quick scoring chances.

Fun Activity: Fast Break Drill

Set up a fast break drill with your team. Divide into two teams and practice running fast breaks against each other. This will improve your speed, passing, and finishing skills.

3-Point Shots

Creating open looks for 3-point shots.

Fun Activity: 3-Point Shooting Contest

Organize a 3-point shooting contest with your friends. Set a timer and see who can make the most 3-point shots within the time limit. This will help improve your long-range shooting accuracy.

Layups

Finishing layups in traffic.

Fun Activity: Layup Relay Race

Set up a relay race where players must dribble down the court and make a layup before passing the ball to the next player. This will improve your layup skills and add a fun, competitive element to your practice.

Mid-Range Shots

Getting open for mid-range shots.

Fun Activity: Knockout

Play a game of Knockout with friends. Line up at the free-throw line and take turns shooting. If the player behind you makes their shot before you, you're out. This will help improve your shooting under pressure.
